\section{Related Work}

\paragraph{Streaming Video Understanding.}
Video-LLMs have progressed from short offline clips to long-video and temporal reasoning benchmarks, where the full video is typically available before inference~\citep{mvbench2024,longvideobench2024,videomme2025}.
Recent online benchmarks instead evaluate prefix-only answering, timestamped queries, and temporal multi-turn interaction over a stream~\citep{ovobench2025,ovbench2025,svbench2025}.
Streaming systems further adapt Video-LLMs to continuous inputs through response-state modeling and feedback policies~\citep{videollm_online2024,dispider2025}.
Other work studies proactive interaction and streaming instruction data for online video dialogue~\citep{streambridge2025,streamo2026}.
These works establish when a model should answer from the observed prefix.
Our focus is complementary: later turns may require source-linked evidence that has already left the active visual window.

\paragraph{Long-Stream Memory.}
Another line of work reduces the cost of long or unbounded video streams.
Visual-token and hierarchical compression reduce dense video context~\citep{longvu2025,videochatflash2026}.
Streaming inference methods maintain compact KV states or recurrent windows~\citep{streamingvlm2026,flashvstream2025}.
Event-memory methods store persistent event structures~\citep{streamforest2025}.
These mechanisms keep long streams tractable, but their histories are usually optimized for forward continuation.
They are less suited to later questions that need to identify when an earlier detail occurred and reload the corresponding visual evidence.
Watch-Think-Interact therefore makes compact memory time-indexed and recallable.

\paragraph{Agentic Multimodal Reasoning.}
Agentic multimodal models treat evidence acquisition as part of the reasoning policy, using tools or additional visual inspection during inference~\citep{deepeyes2026,deepeyesv22026}.
Memory-augmented agents similarly learn to update or revisit compact memories for long-context reasoning~\citep{memagent2026,rememr12026}.
Watch-Think-Interact brings these ideas into streaming video by jointly deciding whether to wait, answer, recall time-bounded history, or transfer compact state as chunks arrive online.
