\section*{Limitations}

WTI makes long streams tractable with compact memory and time-bounded recall, but these operations are not lossless.
Imprecise memory lines or recall ranges may still miss small objects, brief actions, or subtle temporal order.

Our experiments use chunked streams and public benchmarks.
Although they cover Real-Time, Backward, Forward, and long-video reasoning, they do not fully capture high frame rates, multi-camera input, noisy user queries, audio-rich scenes, or safety-critical deployment.
Future work should measure latency, memory growth, interruptions, and longer live-session failures.

Verifier rewards may inherit data-construction errors; improving robustness remains important.
