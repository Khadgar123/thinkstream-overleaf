\section*{Limitations}

WTI makes long streams tractable through compact memory and time-bounded recall, but these operations are not lossless.
Memory compression may omit small objects, brief actions, counts, or subtle temporal order, and imprecise recall may miss supporting evidence.

Our experiments use 1-second chunks at 2 FPS and public benchmark-style trajectories.
They cover Real-Time, Backward, Forward, and long-video reasoning, but not high-frame-rate motion, audio-rich scenes, multi-camera input, noisy queries, interruptions, or long live sessions.

Stream-GDPO requires online rollouts in a long-context environment, increasing training cost and engineering complexity.
Scaling to longer streams, larger models, or richer tools will require better memory, rollout, and inference efficiency.

Finally, WTI-82K and verifier rewards may inherit errors from generated questions, causal alignment, answer verification, or teacher trajectories.
Real deployment also requires stronger human evaluation, privacy filtering, and safety constraints for sensitive settings.
