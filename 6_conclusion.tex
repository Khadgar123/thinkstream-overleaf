\section{Conclusion}

We presented Watch-Think-Interact, a framework for long-horizon multi-turn streaming video reasoning under bounded context.
WTI couples active-window perception, compact time-indexed memory, time-bounded recall, and response timing in one online policy trained with ChronoInteract-82K and Stream-GDPO.
Experiments and ablations show that this coupling improves history-dependent reasoning while preserving Real-Time and Forward performance across streaming regimes.
