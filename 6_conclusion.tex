\section{Conclusion}

We presented Watch-Think-Interact (WTI), a framework for long-horizon multi-turn streaming video reasoning under causal timing and bounded context.
WTI treats streaming interaction as a single online policy that couples active-window perception, compact time-indexed memory, time-bounded recall, and response timing.
To train this behavior, we construct WTI-82K and optimize rollouts with Stream-GDPO, aligning supervised initialization with the same chunk-level environment used at inference.
Across StreamingBench, OVO-Bench, and offline long-video benchmarks, WTI improves history-dependent reasoning while preserving Real-Time, Forward, and general long-video performance.
These results suggest that recallable compact memory is a practical path toward streaming Video-LLMs that can reason over evidence after it leaves the active visual window.
